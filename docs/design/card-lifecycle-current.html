How a picture card lives

One frame, from the moment something asks for it to the moment it is on the glass. Times are measured on dev-0005, 2026-09-26 and 2026-09-27.

What happens

Every frame takes the same road

  • A tap, or a 15-minute timer, wakes the refresher.
  • The face fetches and draws an SVG; resvg rasterises it.
  • The server encodes RLE565 and takes the SHA-256 that names it.
  • It goes over the wire in 1,920-byte chunks, one ack each.
  • PushScene names the digest; the panel draws it.

Where it lands

Flash, always

  • AssetBegin carries volatile: false, hardcoded.
  • The commit erases and writes flash: 1.5 s.
  • AssetRelease compacts the blob region: 9.4 s.
  • Drawing reads it back mmap'd, so it costs no RAM.

What it buys

Nothing

  • The config declares assets: [] — no fonts, no icons.
  • Frames change every 15 min, so every digest is new.
  • After 21 h away, all four re-transferred in full.
  • Written, compacted, superseded — never read.
