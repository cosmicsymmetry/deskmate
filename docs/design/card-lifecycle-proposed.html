Deskmate · Track C1 · proposed

Every view, already there

The same frame, with the expensive work moved off the interactive path. Every view a face offers is drawn ahead of time and kept resident in PSRAM, so the tap itself carries no picture — it only names one the panel is already holding. The sixteen slots are a shared pool — fifteen resident plus one arriving — so a card may offer two views or fifteen.

What changes

A face declares its views

  • However many it wants — two for weather, a page per screenful for a feed.
  • A pure onTap(state, event) → viewId keeps tap meaning in the face, with no fetch and no draw.
  • Every view is pushed volatile: true and stays resident.
  • The 16 slots are one shared pool — fifteen resident plus one arriving — not a per-card ration: three cards of five views, or one card of fifteen.

What it costs

Five megabytes, and more wire

  • 16 × 329,740 B = 5.03 MB of the 7.97 MiB measured free. Leaves ~2.94 MiB.
  • N× the bytes per refresh — entirely off the interactive path.
  • After a power cycle a card waits for its first push instead of showing a day-old frame.
  • Flash goes quiet: it holds nothing today, and stays available if a font or a user image ever needs it.

Why sixteen

Memory, and nothing else

  • 16 × 329,740 B is 5.03 MB — the most that fits with real headroom left.
  • The durable tier is not a reason: nothing declares an asset, and every font the host emits is Baked into firmware.
  • AssetRelease's 32-digest cap is therefore headroom — 16 used, 16 spare should assets ever arrive.
  • Needs a wire-contract amendment, a firmware change, a flash and an OTA re-verification — batched with C2.
